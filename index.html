About

I'm not your average Jo.

Born in China and raised in New York — and yet, the only Mandarin I'm fluent in is the kind you peel for a snack! Growing up between cultures gave me a unique perspective and a deep curiosity about how people experience the world.

As a Product Designer, I channel that curiosity into understanding diverse perspectives and uncovering needs that aren't always obvious. This approach allows me to create solutions that thoughtfully balance both user and business needs.

I thrive in environments where curiosity drives innovation, collaboration is the norm, and shared problem-solving is a daily practice. These are the spaces where I can design experiences that are intuitive, seamless, and anticipate problems users didn't even know they had.

Based in NYC with a background in marketing and advertising, I bring 6+ years of experience designing systems across complex industries, including restaurant technology, finance, and healthcare.

I'm a builder at heart—drawn to real-world problems and driven to test ideas quickly. I discovered my passion for product design in 2018 after independently developing and validating an app concept, using experimentation and data to guide decisions—and the rest is history. That mindset still defines my approach today: a curiosity beyond the interface, informed by human psychology, systems thinking, and unexpected domains to create thoughtful, intuitive, and impactful solutions.
